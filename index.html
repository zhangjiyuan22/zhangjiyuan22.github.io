Adaptive computation

AdaMGrid

Replaces repeated numerical calculations with reusable pre-computed adaptive maps and interpolation, refining resolution only where local errors require it.

Enables the previously prohibitive triple-lens full parameter-space search.

Algorithms · C++

FastVBLensing

Develops the augmented priority queue and uses the skip list to remove a quadratic bottleneck in contour-integration software.

More than 3× end-to-end acceleration without loss of accuracy.

Physics-informed inference

MetalLens

Combines multi-band photometry, microlensing geometry, and stellar models to break otherwise limiting parameter degeneracies.

Opens a route to metallicity measurements for M dwarfs out to the Galactic Bulge.
