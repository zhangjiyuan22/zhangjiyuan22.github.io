Jiyuan Zhang 张纪元

Building new capabilities for physical inference.

I am a PhD candidate in Astronomy at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Shude Mao, and hold a B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering and Automation, also from Tsinghua University.

My research develops the measurement and computational capabilities needed to turn Roman's microlensing census into demographic studies of cold planetary systems—how planet occurrence depends on host composition, and how cold planets are organized in multi-planet and stellar-binary systems. More broadly, I design efficient algorithms for complex physical inference, from low-level data structures to adaptive computation and cluster-scale searches.

Research program

Host metallicity and system multiplicity of cold planets in the Roman era

Read the scientific story →

Host metallicity and system multiplicity carry complementary information about planet formation, yet both remain poorly constrained for low-mass planets beyond the snow line. Microlensing reaches this population, and Roman will provide the statistical sample. My research develops two currently missing capabilities needed to turn that sample into demographic constraints on how cold-planet formation depends on host composition and system architecture.
